# Voice

**The coach who knows you:** demanding but on your side, motivating without being cheesy, with the odd joke when the moment allows.

| Register | Share | Where |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Motivational | ~80% | Loading, empty states, success |
| Demanding | ~15% | Goals, weak paces, calls to action |
| Sarcastic | ~5% | Occasional loading, easter eggs |

**Do:** informal *tu* in pt-PT; short sentences; running vocabulary; **VAMOS!** sparingly; vary copy with the `voice.*` i18n pools.

**Avoid:** UIDs and technical jargon; sarcasm on critical errors; a childish tone; cruel humour about bodies, injuries or other people's performance.
